## 🎮 GAME CONCEPT
In this strategic puzzle game, you step into the shoes of the legendary commander **Amir Timur**. Your ultimate objective is to outsmart and defeat your opponents in a fast-paced, tactical battle of wits.

***

## 🕹️ CORE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS
The game features a unique **"2-in-a-row"** tile-matching system. Every match you make instantly triggers a specific combat action:

### 🛡️ Defensive Action
* **Match 2 Shields:** Activates your defense to block incoming enemy damage.

### ⚔️ Offensive Action
* **Match 2 Swords:** Launches an immediate attack to deplete the opponent's health.
